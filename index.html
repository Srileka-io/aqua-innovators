🔬 Research & Scientific Foundation

The questions behind Aqua Innovators.

🌿 What is Transpiration?

Plants absorb water through their roots and release water vapour mainly through tiny openings called stomata. This natural process is called transpiration.

🔬 Stephen Hales — 1727

Stephen Hales carried out early quantitative experiments on plant water movement and transpiration, helping establish scientific study of water loss from plants.

🌱 Which Plants?

We aim to experimentally compare plant species and identify which plants and conditions provide higher recoverable transpiration.

🌡️ Does Climate Matter?

Yes. Transpiration varies with temperature, humidity, sunlight, wind, soil moisture and plant species. Our system therefore uses adaptive monitoring.

🛡️ Will We Damage the Plant?

Our goal is not to extract all of the plant's water. The proposed system prioritizes plant health and can reduce or pause recovery when stress conditions occur.

💧 Why Micro/Nano Channels?

Hydrophilic microchannels or nanostructured surfaces can be explored to guide condensed moisture efficiently. They are an area for future optimization, not a claim that we extract water directly from inside the plant.
